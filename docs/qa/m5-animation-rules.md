# M5 — Animation and the rest of the rules

Moves slide for 220ms with an ease-out curve. Captures leave a fading ghost. Reduced motion snaps. Castling, en passant, and promotion keep the moving piece's id. Promotion opens a dialog that Escape does not dismiss. Keys 1–4 and Q, R, B, N choose the piece.

Check draws a red rim on the king. Threefold and fifty-move open a claim. Mate, stalemate, and insufficient material end the game and lock moves. Undo, scoresheet navigation, and restart stay available.
