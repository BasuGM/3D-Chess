# M4 — Pieces and input

All 32 pieces share six Staunton geometries. Clicking a piece selects it. Clicking or dragging onto a legal square moves. An illegal click does not change the scoresheet. Keyboard play of scholar's mate, an illegal pawn push, castling, and promotion is covered by Playwright.

The starting position matches the FEN. Knights face the camera so the silhouette reads from White's and Black's seats.
